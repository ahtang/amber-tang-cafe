# Amber Café chatbot — interview questionnaire

Answer conversationally in your own words. One or two paragraphs each is plenty; specific examples and phrases you actually use are more useful than polished résumé language. Skip anything you don't want public.

## Your voice and background
1. Give your natural 30-second answer to “Tell me about yourself.”
2. What are three words or expressions you use a lot? What phrases would you never say?
3. How would you describe your strengths without sounding like a résumé?
4. What kind of work energizes you, and what kind drains you?
5. What three misconceptions about you or your design style would you correct?

## Project stories
6. Which project are you most proud of, and why *personally*?
7. Walk me through that project's problem, your specific decisions, roadblocks, and outcomes.
8. Tell me about a design you changed substantially after new user evidence.
9. Tell me about a time data disagreed with interviews or stakeholder opinions.
10. What project didn't go as planned? What did you learn?
11. What measurable impact can you confidently attribute to your own work, versus a broader team?

## Behavioral interview stories
12. Describe a disagreement with a PM or engineer. How did you resolve it?
13. Tell me about influencing a decision without formal authority.
14. Describe making a tradeoff between speed, scope, and craft.
15. Tell me about ambiguous requirements or a project with no clear owner.
16. Describe a research finding that changed the product roadmap.
17. Tell me about mentoring, design critique, or helping someone on your team grow.
18. What's constructive feedback you've received and acted on?
19. What would you do differently if you could repeat a shipped project?

## Senior product design judgment
20. How do you decide whether a problem deserves research, experimentation, or a fast design fix?
21. How do you define success before designing? What metrics do you trust?
22. How do you balance end-user needs with company growth goals?
23. What is your process from messy discovery through launch and post-launch evaluation?
24. How do you handle a technically infeasible but user-loved concept?
25. What does high-quality interaction and visual craft mean to you?
26. How do you prioritize accessibility, edge cases, and design-system consistency?
27. What separates a senior designer from a mid-level designer in your view?

## Portfolio and career
28. Why HealthSherpa, Calendly, and UKG? What threads connect your work?
29. What do you want a hiring manager to remember after visiting?
30. Why did you build a café portfolio, and what did you learn building it?
31. What's the best way for someone to explore your projects?
32. What roles, industries, and team cultures are you most interested in next?

## Example responses / voice samples
33. How would you respond casually to someone asking “What's your favorite case study?”
34. How would you answer “Are you more of a growth designer or product designer?”
35. How do you joke about the amount of effort you put into this café?

## Grounding rules
Only publish information you approve for public visitors. Mark any confidential examples as private. For each story note: situation, your role, decisions, collaborators, result, and what you learned. A future hosted AI version should cite these approved sources, avoid invented metrics, and be clearly labeled as an AI representation rather than a direct conversation with you.
